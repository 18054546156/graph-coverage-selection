## KEY METHODOLOGICAL INSIGHTS

### Common Themes Across Papers:

1. **Coreset Theory:** All k-center papers (2016-2026) emphasize sublinear size guarantees while maintaining approximation bounds.

2. **Instance Weighting:** Recent papers (2023-2024) combine learned weights with soft labels for robustness to noisy labels.

3. **Active Learning:** Shift toward batch-mode and compute-efficient approaches (2021-2024).

4. **Curriculum Learning:** Dynamic difficulty estimation replacing fixed heuristics (2020+).

5. **Soft Labels:** Emerging as key technique for handling label uncertainty and noisy supervision (2024).
