# Pre-registration: S1b replication + S2 colour-aug confound check (2026-10-02, written before any S1b/S2 result)

Context: S1 (blood+path, seeds 42-46, 9 methods) passed H1 (tau_EXP<tau_STR 4/4, #1 flip 4/4), but
(a) the within-seed EXP reshuffle is about as large as the cross-seed clean reshuffle, and
(b) the only systematic signal is post hoc: the method x EXP interaction
    I(m) = [BA(m) - BA(random)]_EXP - [BA(m) - BA(random)]_clean   (sev 1-3, mean over 5 seeds)
    was negative at 2% and positive at 5% for mv_mean (blood -6.3/+5.0, path -2.8/+4.2),
    and negative at 2% for herding (blood -6.2, path -5.4).

## S1b (organamnist, organsmnist, tissuemnist; inference only; same 9 methods, seeds 42-46)
Shift families for grayscale datasets (MedMNIST-C registry):
  EXP (intensity) = brightness_up/down, contrast_up/down, gamma_corr_up/down (gamma: organ only)
  STR = pixelate, jpeg_compression, gaussian_blur, *_noise
Note: this is intensity/exposure shift, not colour/stain shift -- a generality test, not a stain test.

Primary replication test (R1): predicted sign of I(mv_mean): negative at 2%, positive at 5%.
  REPLICATES if the predicted sign holds in >= 5 of 6 (dataset, ratio) cells.
  Chance level under a symmetric null is 6 * 0.5 = 3; P(>=5 of 6) = 7/64 = 0.11.
Secondary (R2): I(herding) < 0 at 2% in >= 2 of 3 datasets.
Also reported, not gating: H1 gate per dataset (tau_EXP<tau_STR, #1 flip), regret, noise-floor tau.
If R1 fails, the 2%-vs-5% sign pattern is NOT reported as a finding.

## S2 (blood+path, same 180 selections, retrained with ColorJitter(.4,.4,.4,.1), no crop/flip)
Comparison is S2 vs S1 on identical selections and seeds.
  G2-H1:  the H1 gate (tau_EXP<tau_STR >= 3/4 cells AND #1 flip >= 2/4) still passes on S2.
  G2-I:   for mv_mean and herding, |I(m)| in S2 >= 50% of |I(m)| in S1 with the same sign, in >= 3 of 4 cells
          each (pooled: >= 6 of 8 method-cells).
  G2-R:   clean-pick regret under EXP (per-seed mean) in S2 >= 50% of S1's 3.37pp, i.e. >= 1.7pp.
Decision:
  all three pass  -> "selection-method choice matters under acquisition shift even with colour augmentation";
                     proceed to S3 (DRO coreset comparison / robust-selection design).
  G2-H1 or G2-R fail -> the shift problem is largely removed by standard colour augmentation; report as a
                     negative/qualifying result, no new selection method.
  Only G2-I fails -> rank instability persists but the specific interaction does not; S3 only as a
                     benchmark/diagnostic paper, no method claim.
Also reported, not gating: absolute EXP BA recovery per method, clean BA change from augmentation, STR change.

## Shift-size caveat (S0)
S0 compares standardized colour-statistic gaps. If EXP severity 1 already exceeds the real path
train->test (cross-centre) gap, all EXP conclusions are stated as "under synthetic exposure shift larger
than the observed cross-centre shift", and path's real test split (already cross-centre) is reported as
the only realistic-magnitude evidence.
